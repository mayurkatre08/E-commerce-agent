# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

| Method            | Estimated Delivery | Cost                  |
|-------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days  | $5.99 (Free over $50) |
| Express Shipping  | 2–3 business days  | $12.99                |
| Overnight         | Next business day  | $24.99                |

## International Shipping

| Region         | Estimated Delivery  | Cost     |
|----------------|---------------------|----------|
| Canada & Mexico| 7–10 business days  | $14.99   |
| Europe         | 10–14 business days | $19.99   |
| Rest of World  | 14–21 business days | $24.99   |

## Order Processing
- Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST** on business days are processed the same day.
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.
- You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order ships.

## Tracking
- Use the tracking number in your confirmation email on our website or the carrier's website.
- Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after shipment.

## Shipping Restrictions
- We do not ship to P.O. Boxes for Express or Overnight orders.
- Some items cannot be shipped internationally due to customs restrictions.

## Lost or Stolen Packages
If your package is marked delivered but not received, contact us within **5 days** of the delivery date. We will file a claim with the carrier on your behalf.
