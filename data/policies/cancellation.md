# Cancellation Policy

## Order Cancellation Window
- Orders can be cancelled **within 1 hour** of placement at no charge.
- After 1 hour, cancellation is only possible if the order status is still **"processing"**.
- Orders with status **"shipped"** or **"delivered"** cannot be cancelled — you must initiate a return instead.

## How to Cancel
1. Go to **Order History** in your account.
2. Select the order you wish to cancel.
3. Click **Cancel Order** if the option is available.
4. You will receive a cancellation confirmation email within 5 minutes.

## Cancellation via Support
If the cancel button is not available, contact our support team immediately with your order ID. We will attempt to intercept the order before it ships.

## Refund After Cancellation
- Cancellations processed within the window receive a **full refund** to the original payment method.
- Refunds appear within **3–5 business days** depending on your bank.
- If the order was placed using store credit, the credit is reinstated immediately.

## Partial Cancellations
- You may cancel individual items from a multi-item order as long as those items have not shipped.
- Shipping charges are recalculated after partial cancellation.

## Non-Cancellable Orders
- Custom or personalized items cannot be cancelled once production has started.
- Digital gift cards are non-cancellable once issued.
