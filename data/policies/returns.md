# Return Policy

## Overview
We accept returns within **30 days** of the delivery date for most items.

## Eligibility
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached.
- Sale items marked "Final Sale" are **not eligible** for return.
- Swimwear and undergarments are **not eligible** for return due to hygiene reasons.
- Footwear must be returned in the original box.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Log in to your account and go to **Order History**.
2. Select the order and click **Request Return**.
3. Choose the item(s) and reason for return.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (available for US orders).
5. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier location within 7 days.

## Refund Timeline
- Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive the item.
- Original shipping charges are non-refundable.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Damaged or Defective Items
If you received a damaged or defective item, contact support within **48 hours** of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund.
