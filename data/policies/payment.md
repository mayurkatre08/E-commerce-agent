# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal and PayPal Pay Later
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Store Credit / Gift Cards
- Klarna (Buy Now, Pay Later — available for orders over $30)

## Payment Security
- All transactions are encrypted using **TLS 1.3**.
- We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
- Payments are processed through **PCI-DSS Level 1** certified payment processors.

## Billing
- Your card is charged at the time of order placement, not at shipment.
- The billing address must match the address on file with your card issuer.
- If a payment fails, you will be notified by email and given 24 hours to update payment details before the order is cancelled.

## Gift Cards
- Gift cards are valid for **2 years** from the date of purchase.
- Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced without proof of purchase.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash.

## Klarna / Buy Now Pay Later
- Split your purchase into **4 interest-free payments** every 2 weeks.
- Available for US customers only.
- Late payments may incur fees as per Klarna's terms.

## Disputes and Chargebacks
- If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us before filing a chargeback.
- Unresolved disputes can be escalated to your card issuer after 10 business days.
